A dense newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image: 42 stories first reported that day, across 7 regions and 8 fields, with region and field count bars counted from the stories themselves and a by-the-numbers strip (126 drones Ukraine shot down of 141, 99/100 for GTA VI, 145 km/h Cyclone Akvan gusts, 200,000 people Odisha plans to move). Thirteen illustrated lead cards with detailed flat-vector scenes (Iranian drones aimed at the US base at Ali Al Salem in Kuwait, APEC opening in Shenzhen, the COP31 fossil-fuel split, the Gaza truce warning, Bangladesh wage protests, Voyager 1 one light-day out, the Salvadoran helicopter strike on the gang boss's house in Canaan, the Odesa strikes, Ethiopia and Eritrea massing troops, the GTA VI midnight rollout, the Bank of England rate warning, Australia's settler sanctions, Cyclone Akvan), a dotted world map with numbered pins and leader lines for the crowded Levant cluster, 29 one-line briefs, and a date-check footer naming stories left out because they were first reported on 17 November. Every headline and figure was checked against reporting of the day: round 3 fixes the Gulf drones, the Haiti raid, the GTA VI launch, the rate warning, 4 dead in Odesa, about 500 factories shut in Bangladesh, and 145 km/h cyclone gusts. The program draws the whole page itself with a hand-built bitmap font and its own PNG encoder, using only the Python standard library.
